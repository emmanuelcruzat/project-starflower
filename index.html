A strategy game of nations and stars

Found a nation. Weather its crises. Claim the stars.

Project Starflower is a slow-burn strategy game that runs in your browser. Reports arrive while you're away, operations unfold over hours, and every decision leaves its mark on a young nation in a crowded star cluster.

The cluster

One capital. Rival players. Hundreds of stars.

You begin with a single star system and a government of your choosing. Around you lies a cluster of constellations, voids and contested borders, shared with empires run by other players, each with plans of its own. Grow the nation, keep it together, and decide how far it reaches.

Gameplay

Four ways to shape a nation

  1. 01Found
  2. 02Decide
  3. 03Direct
  4. 04Chart

01 · Found

Choose who rules

Name your nation and pick its government: a democracy, a monarchy, a corporate state, a military junta, a technocracy and more. Each starts with its own strengths and its own blind spots.

  • Six statistics, from economy to cohesion
  • A title that changes as your standing does
  • An encyclopedia entry that writes itself

02 · Decide

Answer what the cluster sends you

Incidents arrive every few hours, whether you're watching or not: smugglers, strikes, scandals and strangers at the border. Each one asks you to choose, and every choice moves your nation somewhere.

  • Reports keep arriving while you're away
  • A full queue means news goes unheard
  • Outcomes depend on the nation you've built

03 · Direct

Commit to the long game

Launch operations that run in stages over hours or days, and give orders as each stage ends. Set an ambition for the nation to work toward, and see it through.

  • Up to three operations running at once
  • Decisions at every stage
  • Ambitions that take days to fulfill

04 · Chart

Map a living cluster

Every nation gets its own generated cluster: hundreds of systems in named constellations, joined by hyperlanes and broken by vast voids. Open any system to see its stars and planets, and claim the ones you want.

  • Red dwarfs to hypergiants, white dwarfs to black holes
  • Planets, moons and rings in every system
  • Borders that grow with every claim

Up close

Every star is a system

Open any star on the map to see what circles it, drawn to a squeezed but true scale: dwarfs and giants, ringed worlds and their moons, belts of rubble, and the stranger things stars leave behind when they die.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I play it now?

Yes. Project Starflower is an early prototype, but it's playable today in a desktop browser. Choose Play now to found a nation.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free to play, with nothing to install.

Where is my progress saved?

In the browser you play in. There are no accounts yet, so clearing your browser's site data, or playing on another device, starts a new nation.

Does the game keep going while I'm away?

Yes. Time passes in the real world: incidents arrive and operations progress while the page is closed, and everything catches up when you return.

What's coming next?

More of the cluster: territory that matters, travel between systems, and other players' empires to negotiate with. Accounts will follow, so a nation can travel with you.
